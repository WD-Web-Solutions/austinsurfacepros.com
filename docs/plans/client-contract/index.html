Accepted drafting decisions

Production access

Remove public registration, public accounts, comments, and topic subscriptions. Keep individual admin accounts only for Wayne Filer and authorized employees.

Monthly changes

Include up to five minor content edits totaling 30 minutes per month. Extra work is $75/hour after advance approval. Material work requires a signed change order.

Meetings and support

Meetings are scheduled ad hoc by mutual availability. Up to 60 minutes per month is included; unused time does not roll over. Extra time is $75/hour. WD may waive a charge without creating an ongoing entitlement.

Ownership

The client owns its domain rights, content, logo, and media. WD retains its background tools, reusable code, infrastructure systems, generalized components, and know-how.

Legal risk

Add warranty disclaimers, consequential-damages exclusion, a reasonable liability cap, and narrow reciprocal indemnities. Flag these provisions for Texas-counsel review.

Shared compliance

WD implements agreed technical measures. The client approves business claims, content, privacy positions, and legal requirements. WD does not guarantee general regulatory compliance.

Client dependencies

The client must provide timely approvals, licensed media, content, and domain access. Wayne may text a temporary password if he insists; WD should use it only to establish access, replace it with delegated access where possible, require the client to change it afterward, and enable multifactor authentication. Client-caused delays extend the schedule.

Payment and launch

$300 is due upon signing in August 2026. The $50 monthly fee begins September 1 and is due on the first. It proceeds if production-ready or delayed by the client, but is deferred for a material WD-only delay.

Initial pricing term and renewal

The agreed $50 monthly fee, $75 hourly rate, included allowances, and overage structure remain fixed through August 31, 2027. WD may propose different pricing for a renewal by giving at least 60 days' written notice. New pricing takes effect only through a written renewal or amendment approved by both parties. If the parties do not approve a renewal, neither party is required to continue beyond the initial term, subject to the agreement's transition and domain-transfer provisions.

Domain custody

The domain will be managed inside WD's AWS account, with the client identified contractually as the beneficial owner. WD must transfer it after termination and payment of outstanding balances.

Managed hosting

Use a managed-hosting support schedule rather than uptime credits or 24/7 SLA obligations. Third-party outages and planned maintenance are excluded from response objectives.

Square versus DocuSign

Recommendation: pilot Square Contracts for this agreement and cancel DocuSign only after confirming the completed Square record contains every clause, both parties' signatures, timestamps, and a downloadable final PDF.

  • Square Contracts supports custom templates, electronic initials/signatures, and a finalized PDF.
  • A Square contract can be attached to an invoice. Some paid Square plans can require signature before payment.
  • Square's contract is built from clauses and fields; it will not necessarily preserve the exact visual design of the Word/PDF template.
  • Keep the highlighted DOCX as the internal review copy, then transfer the approved terms into Square and compare them clause-by-clause before sending.

Business-domain email proposition

Recommendation: keep austinsurfacepros@gmail.com as a backup/recovery address, but use one Google Workspace mailbox at the business domain as the primary public address. Start with info@austinsurfacepros.com and add no-cost aliases such as wayne@, estimates@, and billing@ to the same mailbox. Separate private employee mailboxes require additional paid users.

Pros

  • Looks more established and reinforces the Austin Surface Pros name in every message.
  • Keeps business correspondence under the company domain instead of a personal consumer account.
  • Supports properly configured SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication.
  • Makes future employee onboarding and offboarding cleaner through individual accounts or aliases.
  • Wayne can continue using the familiar Gmail interface.

Cons

  • Google Workspace Business Starter has a recurring per-user charge.
  • Setup requires DNS changes and optional migration of existing Gmail messages.
  • Every person who needs a separate private inbox generally requires a separate license.

The optional mailbox subscription should be paid directly by Austin Surface Pros and should not consume the website's $10 infrastructure allowance. WD can include the initial DNS and mailbox configuration in the website setup.
