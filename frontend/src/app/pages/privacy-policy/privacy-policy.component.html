1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Austin Surface Pros ("Austin Surface Pros," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information when you visit austinsurfacepros.com, request an estimate, create or use an account, interact with the blog, or communicate with us about our services. "Personal information" means information that identifies, relates to, or can reasonably be linked with an individual.

This policy does not apply to third-party websites or services that we do not control. It also does not replace any privacy terms contained in a contract that applies to a specific project or business relationship.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you submit an estimate request, we collect your name, email address, the service you are interested in, and the project details you include. You may also choose to provide a company name and phone number. If you continue communicating with us, we collect the contents of those communications and information reasonably needed to prepare an estimate, perform requested services, maintain business records, or support the resulting customer relationship.

When you create an account, we collect your full name, email address, and password. We store a cryptographic hash of the password rather than the password itself. We also maintain an account identifier, role, status, creation date, last-login date, and administrative notes that authorized personnel may add to support or administer the account.

If you comment on a blog post or follow a blog topic, we collect the comment text or selected topic together with your account identifier. Your full name and the date of a comment are displayed publicly with the comment. Blog authors' names are also displayed with their posts. Administrators may upload blog images and create, edit, publish, or remove blog content; uploaded images and associated content are stored for those purposes.

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, financial account credentials, medical information, or other sensitive personal information through the estimate form.

Technical information

Our website and cloud infrastructure necessarily receive limited technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as an IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, request dates and times, and diagnostic or security data. This information may be processed automatically by our hosting and security providers even when it is not retained in an application database.

3. Browser storage and authentication

After registration or login, the site stores an access token and a copy of basic account details—account identifier, full name, email address, and role—in your browser's local storage. The token is sent with authenticated API requests so the site can recognize your session and enforce account permissions. The token has a limited validity period, but the locally stored values may remain until you log out, clear your browser data, or the application removes them.

Do not use the remembered session on a shared or public device. You can remove this browser-stored information by using the Logout control and can also clear it through your browser settings.

4. How we use information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to questions and estimate requests;
  • Evaluating, scheduling, and performing requested services;
  • Communicating about an inquiry, estimate, project, or service;
  • Creating, authenticating, securing, and administering accounts;
  • Publishing and moderating blog posts and comments, displaying author attribution, and maintaining topic subscriptions;
  • Operating, maintaining, troubleshooting, and securing the website;
  • Preventing fraud, misuse, and other harmful activity;
  • Maintaining appropriate business and transaction records; and
  • Complying with law and establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims.

We will not use personal information for a materially different purpose without providing any notice or obtaining any consent required by law.

5. Cookies and online tracking

As of the date shown at the top of this policy, this website does not set cookies or use analytics pixels, advertising trackers, or similar technologies. As described above, signed-in users' access tokens and basic account details are stored in local storage for authentication and account operation. We do not use that storage for advertising or cross-site tracking, and we do not use website activity for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising.

Because the site currently uses no non-essential cookies or tracking technologies and does not sell or share website activity for targeted advertising, there is no separate cookie-consent or "Do Not Sell or Share" control. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide consent or opt-out controls before using those technologies when required by applicable law.

Some browsers transmit "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals. Our current practices do not involve tracking, selling, or sharing that would require a different response to those signals.

6. When we disclose information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose the categories of personal information described above to the following categories of recipients, only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Cloud hosting, database, file-storage, information-technology, security, and website maintenance providers that process information on our behalf;
  • Employees, contractors, and subcontractors who need information to evaluate or perform a requested project;
  • Accountants, attorneys, insurers, and other professional advisors;
  • Government authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security; and
  • A buyer, successor, or other relevant party in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not disclose personal information for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising, and we do not use personal information to profile individuals in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Information you intentionally publish through the blog is disclosed to other site visitors. In particular, comments display the comment text, your full name, and the comment date. Published posts display the author's name and any included images or content.

7. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. The appropriate period depends on whether an inquiry results in a project or ongoing business relationship, the nature and sensitivity of the information, our recordkeeping needs, applicable limitation periods, and legal, tax, accounting, insurance, security, and dispute-resolution obligations. When we dispose of personal information, we take reasonable steps to make it unreadable or unrecoverable, as appropriate.

Account information is generally retained while the account remains active and afterward as reasonably needed for security, administration, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Comments remain until they are deleted by the author or an administrator, and topic subscriptions remain until the user unsubscribes or the related account data is removed. Administrative notes and blog content may be retained as business records. Browser-stored session data remains until logout, browser-data clearing, or application removal, although an access token may expire sooner.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, use, modification, or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes. Authentication tokens are stored in the signed-in user's browser, so users should log out on shared devices and protect access to their browsers and devices.
