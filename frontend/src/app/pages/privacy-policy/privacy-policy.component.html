The short version

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Austin Surface Pros ("Austin Surface Pros," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information when you visit austinsurfacepros.com, request an estimate, create or use an account, interact with the blog, or communicate with us about our services. "Personal information" means information that identifies, relates to, or can reasonably be linked with an individual.

This policy does not apply to third-party websites or services that we do not control. It also does not replace any privacy terms contained in a contract that applies to a specific project or business relationship.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you submit an estimate request, we collect your name, property type, project address, requested service, desired timeline, and the project details you include. You must provide at least one contact method—an email address or phone number—and may provide both. You may also choose to provide a company name. If you continue communicating with us, we collect the contents of those communications and information reasonably needed to prepare an estimate, perform requested services, maintain business records, or support the resulting customer relationship.

The optional photo step lets you select up to three project photos to organize before follow-up. Those selected photos remain on your device and are not uploaded or submitted with the estimate request. We may ask you to share photos later through an agreed contact method.

When you create an account, we collect your full name, email address, and password. We store a cryptographic hash of the password rather than the password itself. We also maintain an account identifier, role, status, creation date, last-login date, and administrative notes that authorized personnel may add to support or administer the account.

If you comment on a blog post or follow a blog topic, we collect the comment text or selected topic together with your account identifier. Your full name and the date of a comment are displayed publicly with the comment. Blog authors' names are also displayed with their posts. Production posts use the signed-in administrator's account name for that attribution. The browser-local demo uses the fixed name "Austin Surface Pros" instead of collecting a separate author name. Administrators may upload blog images and create, edit, publish, or remove blog content; uploaded images and associated content are stored for those purposes.

Authorized administrators may upload project-gallery photos and provide titles, alternative text, descriptions, hashtags, city and state, crop settings, and an optional capture date. A photo may depict identifiable people, vehicles, or property and may therefore contain personal information. The gallery studio reads IPTC or XMP city, state, and capture-date metadata on the administrator's device to prefill editable fields. Production uploads are sent directly to private Amazon S3 staging storage through a short-lived upload link. Our backend validates the image, removes embedded metadata, creates compressed display and thumbnail copies, and publishes the approved photo together with the title, description, hashtags, city, and state.

Service-area map and estimate address checker

The services page displays an interactive map using OpenStreetMap data. Loading and using the map causes your browser to request map tiles from the OpenStreetMap Foundation, which receives technical information such as your IP address, browser information, referring page, requested map tiles, and request date and time.

After you enter four characters in either address checker, the address text you type is sent to Komoot's Photon geocoding service to return suggested locations. When you select a suggestion, the website uses the returned coordinates in browser memory to estimate distance from central Austin. The services page also displays the location on the map. Austin Surface Pros does not save the search text, coordinates, or coverage result in browser storage or send them to its application server. On the estimate page, the selected street address, city, state, and ZIP code are added to the form and sent to Austin Surface Pros only if you submit the estimate request. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information in either address field.

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, financial account credentials, medical information, or other sensitive personal information through the estimate form.

Technical information

Our website and cloud infrastructure necessarily receive limited technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as an IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, request dates and times, and diagnostic or security data. This information may be processed automatically by our hosting and security providers even when it is not retained in an application database.

3. Browser storage and authentication

After registration or login, the site stores an access token and a copy of basic account details—account identifier, full name, email address, and role—in your browser's local storage. The token is sent with authenticated API requests so the site can recognize your session and enforce account permissions. The token has a limited validity period, but the locally stored values may remain until you log out, clear your browser data, or the application removes them.

Do not use the remembered session on a shared or public device. You can remove this browser-stored information by using the Logout control and can also clear it through your browser settings.

Local demo content studios and browser search

In development and demo builds, the blog content studio uses IndexedDB to store posts, drafts, hashtags, uploaded thumbnail images, images inserted into articles, and cached article embeddings on the device where they were created. A sessionStorage value remembers the mock administrator's signed-in state for the current browser tab. These browser-local features do not send blog content or mock credentials to Austin Surface Pros' application database.

The browser downloads a compact open-source text-embedding model from this website's own origin. The model may be retained by the browser's HTTP cache or Cache API to improve later load times. Article text and search phrases are processed locally and are not transmitted to Hugging Face, an AI provider, or another search service.

Local demo content remains until it is deleted through the content studio, replaced with the demo seeds, or removed by clearing this site's browser data. Closing the tab removes the mock login session but does not delete saved posts. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to view local demo content, so do not place confidential or personal information in demo posts.

The demo project-gallery studio similarly stores uploaded photo files, crops, titles, alternative text, descriptions, hashtags, and locations in IndexedDB on that device. Demo gallery content is not sent to Austin Surface Pros, Amazon S3, or another image-processing service. It remains until removed in the studio, replaced with the demo seeds, or cleared through browser settings. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to view it.

5. Cookies and online tracking

As of the date shown at the top of this policy, this website does not set cookies or use analytics pixels, advertising trackers, or similar technologies. As described above, signed-in users' access tokens and basic account details are stored in local storage for authentication and account operation. Demo-only IndexedDB, sessionStorage, and same-origin model caching provide local blog and gallery editing and search. We do not use that storage for advertising or cross-site tracking, and we do not use website activity for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising.

Because the site currently uses no non-essential cookies or tracking technologies and does not sell or share website activity for targeted advertising, there is no separate cookie-consent or "Do Not Sell or Share" control. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide consent or opt-out controls before using those technologies when required by applicable law.

Some browsers transmit "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals. Our current practices do not involve tracking, selling, or sharing that would require a different response to those signals.

7. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. The appropriate period depends on whether an inquiry results in a project or ongoing business relationship, the nature and sensitivity of the information, our recordkeeping needs, applicable limitation periods, and legal, tax, accounting, insurance, security, and dispute-resolution obligations. When we dispose of personal information, we take reasonable steps to make it unreadable or unrecoverable, as appropriate.

Account information is generally retained while the account remains active and afterward as reasonably needed for security, administration, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Comments remain until they are deleted by the author or an administrator, and topic subscriptions remain until the user unsubscribes or the related account data is removed. Administrative notes and blog content may be retained as business records. Browser-stored session data remains until logout, browser-data clearing, or application removal, although an access token may expire sooner.

Browser-local demo blog data follows the deletion behavior described in Section 3. Deleting a local post also deletes images embedded in that post, and changing local content clears its saved search embeddings so stale vectors are not retained.

Production gallery photos and their metadata remain while they are published or otherwise reasonably needed as content and business records. Removing a gallery photo through the studio removes its database record and staged and processed objects. Abandoned production staging objects are scheduled to expire after one day. Cached copies may persist temporarily in content-delivery or browser caches. Browser-local demo gallery data follows the deletion behavior described in Section 3.

Photos selected in the estimate form remain in the current browser page only and are discarded when you remove them, refresh or close the page, or submit the form. They are not transferred to Austin Surface Pros by the photo-selection control.

Address-checker search text, suggested locations, coordinates, and coverage results are held only in the current page's browser memory by Austin Surface Pros and are discarded when the page is refreshed or closed. If you submit an estimate request, its selected project address is retained with the request as described above. OpenStreetMap Foundation and Komoot may process or retain request and technical data under their own privacy notices and operational requirements.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, use, modification, or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes. Authentication tokens are stored in the signed-in user's browser, so users should log out on shared devices and protect access to their browsers and devices.

Gallery upload links expire after a short period and are limited to a private staging object. Only administrators can create, complete, edit, reorder, or delete production gallery entries. Uploaded bytes are checked for supported format, file size, pixel dimensions, and successful image decoding before publication; processed copies omit embedded photo metadata.
