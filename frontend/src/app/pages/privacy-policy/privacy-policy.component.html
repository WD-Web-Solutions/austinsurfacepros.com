Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

The short version

  • We collect information you submit when requesting an estimate or contacting us.
  • The demo blog studio stores its posts, uploaded images, and administrator session only in your browser.
  • Blog search runs on your device; search terms and article text are not sent to an AI provider.
  • We do not sell personal information or use website activity for targeted advertising.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

When you submit an estimate or contact request, we collect your name, email address, service interest, and project details. You may also provide a company name and phone number. Please do not submit government identifiers, account credentials, medical information, or other sensitive information.

Technical information

Our hosting and security infrastructure necessarily receives limited technical data needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic data.

2. Local demo blog storage and search

In the demo environment, the blog content studio uses IndexedDB to store posts, drafts, hashtags, uploaded thumbnail images, images inserted into articles, and cached article embeddings on the device where they were created. A sessionStorage value remembers the demo administrator’s signed-in state for the current browser tab. These features do not send the content or credentials to Austin Surface Pros.

The browser downloads a compact, open-source text-embedding model from Austin Surface Pros’ own website. The model may be retained by the browser’s HTTP Cache or Cache API to improve later load times. Article text and search phrases are processed locally in the browser and are not transmitted to Hugging Face, an AI provider, or another search service.

Local demo content remains until it is deleted through the content studio, replaced using the demo reset control, or removed by clearing this site’s browser data. Closing the tab removes the demo login session but does not delete locally saved posts. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to view local demo content, so do not place confidential or personal information in demo posts.

3. How we use information

  • Responding to questions and estimate requests;
  • Evaluating, scheduling, and performing requested services;
  • Operating, troubleshooting, and securing the website;
  • Maintaining appropriate business records; and
  • Complying with law and protecting rights, safety, and security.

4. Cookies and online tracking

We do not currently use advertising pixels, cross-site behavioral tracking, session replay, or analytics cookies. The local demo storage and same-origin model cache described above are functional technologies used only to provide the content-studio and search features. They are not used to track visitors across websites or for advertising.

Because we do not sell or share website activity for targeted advertising, there is no separate “Do Not Sell or Share” control. If our practices change, we will update this policy and implement consent, opt-out, and Global Privacy Control handling where required before enabling new tracking.

Browser-local demo data follows the deletion behavior described in Section 2. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission, storage system, or browser profile can be guaranteed completely secure.
